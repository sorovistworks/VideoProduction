# dadi.approved Reel 01: "Dadi vs White Rice" (Hinglish, India)

Educational swap: white rice → brown, red, black rice, including white rice's side effects. It ends with a comment call-to-action ("RICE") and a cliffhanger into the next episode (Dadi's chai). Vertical 9:16, about **47 s**, 15 cuts including the black end card, three characters (Dadi, Rohan, Papa), Hinglish dialogue generated inside the clips by Veo 3.1. Music, on-screen text, captions and the black-screen sound effects are added later in the HyperFrames edit, so every clip must have **no music and no text**.

## Dadi's persona (applies to every reel)

**Rich mentality, polished, grounded, humble, experienced, witty, quirky, savage, humorous, nonchalant, never cringe.**

- **She never gives orders.** No "ruk ja", no "khao", no shouting. She states things as someone who has seen it all, and the facts land because she is calm about them.
- **She is unbothered.** Seated, chai in a fine gold-rimmed porcelain cup, minimal movement, perfect comic timing. Rohan is the energetic one; the contrast is the joke.
- **Her savage lines are dry, never mean.** The burn is in how little effort she puts in.
- **She says less than Rohan.** Short, polished lines. She lets a sip of chai, or a stare, do the talking.

## Shot grammar (user's direction)

- **Conversation:** close-ups of whoever is speaking (Rohan or Dadi), or the wide two-shot with the **rice mountain in frame** when both need to be seen.
- **B-roll cutaways:** the rice explanation and "Wahi, jo hum khaate the" cut away to the jars.
- **"Taste toh ganda…":** zooms to **only Rohan**, and a spoon comes in from nowhere.
- **The comment CTA:** zooms to **only Dadi**.

## Pacing (measured from the references)

The reference reels cut every **1.9 s on average** (8 shots in 16.4 s, 10 shots in 19.1 s). Here, cuts are 0.8–4 s, with only the comment CTA held longer. The conversation never pauses, and the next cut lands on the last word. Dadi's delivery stays crisp and effortless.

**Fact check behind the lines:**
- **Milling:** polishing removes the bran and germ, which carry most of the fibre, magnesium and B vitamins.
- **Red rice:** gets its colour from antioxidant pigments. Kerala's Palakkadan Matta is a GI-tagged red rice.
- **Black rice:** has the most anthocyanins (the blueberry pigment) of any rice. It's called "forbidden rice" after the legend of it being reserved for Chinese royalty. Manipur's Chak-hao is GI-tagged.
- **Diabetes link:** daily heavy white-rice intake is linked to higher type 2 diabetes risk (BMJ 2012 meta-analysis, Hu et al.).
