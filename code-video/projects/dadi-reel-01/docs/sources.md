# Sources: reel 1 "Dadi vs White Rice"

Checked 2026-11-05. On-screen claims are kept soft (risk, not cause; "swap", not cure). Not medical advice.

| On-screen claim | Source | Notes from the fact-check |
|---|---|---|
| Polish = bran + fibre gone; brown rice keeps bran → more fibre + magnesium | USDA FoodData Central (cooked long-grain brown vs white rice); summary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_rice , https://www.healthline.com/health/food-nutrition/brown-rice-vs-white-rice | ~84 mg vs ~19 mg magnesium per cooked cup; 1–3 g more fibre. Milling removes bran + germ. White rice is often fortified, but magnesium is not added back. |
| Sugar jaldi upar / bhookh jaldi wapas | General glycaemic-index knowledge (white rice is high-GI) | Shown as Dadi's point, no number on screen. |
| Diabetes ka risk (daily heavy white rice) | Hu EA, Pan A, Malik V, Sun Q. *White rice consumption and risk of type 2 diabetes: meta-analysis and systematic review.* BMJ 2012;344:e1454. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22422870/ · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3307808 | Observational: RR 1.55 (highest vs lowest intake) in Asian populations; +11% per daily serving. Association, not causation (on screen: "observational"). A BMJ letter questioned outcome ascertainment. |
| Kerala ka Palakkadan Matta (GI) | GI Registry of India, application 36: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/36 | Registered; filed 2005, granted ~2007–08 (sources differ on the year, so no year shown). |
| Laal rang = antioxidants | Red pericarp pigments (proanthocyanidins) | Kept generic. |
| Manipur ka Chak-Hao (GI) | GI granted 2020: https://blog.mygov.in/gi-tagged-agricultural-products-of-manipur/ · https://www.outlooktraveller.com/travelnews/story/70230/manipur-black-rice-gets-gi-tag | |
| Anthocyanin se bhara | Manipur govt page on Chakhao: https://momamanipur.mn.gov.in/?p=167 | "Highest of any rice" could not be verified, so it is NOT claimed on screen. |
| "Forbidden rice" ki kahani | Popular legend (reserved for Chinese royalty) | Shown as "kahani" (story), not as history. |
