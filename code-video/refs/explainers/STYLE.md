# Explainers house style: the @healthbyvipul flat-vector health explainer

Studied 2026-11-13 from the account's top 4 reels: frying oil 5.0M views, muscle 3.6M, ear buds 3.4M, BP 2.9M. The media is local only, in `S:\GitHub Repo\dadi.approved\reference\healthbyvipul\`, and the full teardown with timestamps is `teardown.md` there. Take the grammar, never the content.

## Look
- Flat 2D vector with soft gradients and gentle inner shading, no outlines or only thin darker ones. Rounded, friendly proportions with big heads and simple dot eyes. Rosy cheek circles.
- **Objects get faces:** a molecule pill, the BP numbers 120 and 80 as blocks with eyes, earwax in a police cap. Two dot eyes and a small mouth; they react (worried, angry, proud).
- **Two stages:**
  1. Story sets: a kitchen, gym, bathroom, clinic or bedroom, drawn as flat walls with one window, a shelf and a counter. Warm cream walls (#f6e3c4 to #f3d2a8), wood counter (#b5713f / #8a4f2a).
  2. The bokeh stage: a soft vertical gradient, peach (#fbe1c8 → #f2b88a), sky blue (#e8f1fb → #c9dcf1) or maroon (#4a1426 → #2a0a17) for inside the body, with 8 to 14 large soft white circles at 8–20% opacity drifting slowly.
- **Colour means something:** red #e2453c = bad/high, green #2fa66a = good/allowed, purple #6a4be0 = medical term, yellow #f6c343 = a quantity, blue #3b7be0 = the second item.
- A small handle watermark centred at about 78% of the height, low opacity. For Dadi: `@dadi.approved`.

## Type and chips
- **No captions and no headlines.** Words on screen are only short labels.
- **Chip:** a rounded pill with a white fill and bold dark (#2a2a2a) lowercase sans, about 34–44 px tall at 1080 wide, with a soft drop shadow. It sits next to the thing it names. Enter: scale 0 → 1.08 → 1 with a spring over about 0.25 s. Hold 1–2 s. Exit: quick fade/scale down. Coloured variants have a filled pill with white text.
- Numbers as big rounded blocks (Baloo/Fredoka-like heavy rounded font).
- Icons: a red ✗ circle, a green ✓ circle, the orange `?` in a white bubble, numbered green circles, a magnifier, a calendar, a clock. All are drawn, never emoji.

## Pacing and camera
- Something new pops on screen every 1–2 s, and the setting changes every 5–10 s. Hard cuts are rare (0–17 in a whole reel).
- Transitions: **zoom-through** a magnifier lens into the inside view; **slide/whip** to the next stage with speed streaks; **pop-in builds** where items appear one by one; a **rotate** of the whole stage (lying down = turn 90°).
- The camera always drifts: a slow push-in or pan of 2–5% per shot. Characters have idle bobs and blinks.
- Story order: everyday habit → `?` → go inside → what goes wrong (red appears only here) → evidence prop → the rule in numbers → back to the opening scene done right / a ✓✗ summary build.

## Sound
- A calm, clear narrator speaking fast and evenly: about 5.3–5.5 syllable nuclei/s (envelope-peak count), short pauses (median 0.23 s, p75 0.27 s, max about 1 s), talking about 85% of the time.
- Two of the four use no music at all, just voice plus small SFX (pop on chips, whoosh on moves, a ding on ✓).

## Avoid
- Captions, title cards, emoji, 3D, photoreal textures, hard flashes and red before the "mistake" beat.
