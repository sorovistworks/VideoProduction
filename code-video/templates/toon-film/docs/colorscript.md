# Colour script (lighting)

One palette per sequence, with the reason. Palettes live in `film/colorscript.js`; blends happen at story turns.

| Sequence | Film time | Palette | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold open: the inhale and the gust | 0 – 4.33 s | `evening`: violet sky, amber sun low and to camera-right, one amber window | warm, cosy, about to go wrong |
| Time-freeze fly-in | 4.33 – 8.33 s | `freeze`: cool, desaturated, stronger vignette | the world holds its breath; snaps in 0.25 s on the record scratch |
| Rewind and calm | 8.33 s – end | back to `evening` over 0.6 s | "let's back up": we are home again |
